## Meeting Notes  

#### Sprint Three Progress and Review  
- Sprint Three is wrapping up, with the submission deadline set for May 27, 2024.  
- Approximately 10 submissions have been received across various challenges.  
- Missions will be reviewed by the team, and winners will be announced by May 29, 2024.  
- Rewards for Sprint Three will be calculated and distributed by May 31, 2024, after participants submit their preferred Akt addresses.  

#### Akash Accelerate Event and Watch Party  
- The Akash Accelerate event is scheduled to take place in Austin, Texas, on May 28, 2024.  
- A community watch party will be hosted by Robert Del Rey, with attendees gathering to live stream the event and discuss Akash-related topics.  

#### Program Recap and Future Planning  
- A detailed recap of the Zealy program's performance will begin in mid-June.  
- Discussions will focus on lessons learned from the initial pilot and Sprint Three, with an emphasis on improving future campaigns.  
- Feedback from participants and the team will guide the design of Zealy 2.0.  

#### Galxe as an Alternative Platform  
- Galxe is being considered as an alternative to Zealy for mission management and community engagement.  
- Initial impressions:  
  - Galxe requires a one-time setup fee of approximately $700 USD (200 Galxe tokens).  
  - Zealy offers monthly subscription plans starting at $250 USD.  
- Key considerations:  
  - Galxe might offer a larger community and enhanced exposure.  
  - Concerns exist about whether rewards can be paid directly in Akt, as the program’s goal is to introduce participants to Akash tokens.  
- Next steps include creating a test community on Galxe to evaluate the platform’s usability and features before making a final decision.  

#### Participant Questions and Feedback  
- Arsalaan Alam asked about submission deadlines and evaluation criteria for the payment system track.  
  - Submissions will be judged based on functionality, design quality, and originality.  
  - Prototypes and testnet projects are acceptable due to technical constraints like Stripe integration requirements.  
- Encouragement was given to participants to submit high-quality projects even if they do not win, as feedback will help improve future submissions.  

#### Budget and Campaign Finances  
- The Zealy campaign’s wallet currently holds 3,271 Akt, equivalent to $16,500 USD at the time of the meeting.  
- Expenditures so far have been within budget, with funds remaining for future pilot campaigns or potential Galxe testing.
